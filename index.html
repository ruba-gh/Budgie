App Statement

An app that helps adult users with irregular spending maintain financial stability and consistency by categorizing income, tracking daily transactions, and providing real-time affordability insights.

Introduction

Meet Budgie

Managing money is not always difficult because people do not care. It is often difficult because tracking expenses takes effort, spending decisions happen quickly, and many users only notice overspending after the money is already gone.

Budgie was created to support adults in Saudi Arabia who want better financial stability but struggle with consistency, impulsive spending, and manual budgeting.

The goal is to create a simple, low-effort budgeting experience that helps users feel more aware, confident, and in control of their daily spending.

Problem / Opportunity

Budgeting fails at the moment of spending.

The problem

People do not fail at budgeting because they lack financial knowledge. They fail because daily spending is fast, invisible, and often influenced by habits, emotions, and social pressure.

The opportunity

Most financial apps help users pay, save, track, or analyze their money, but they do not support users at the moment when spending decisions happen.

This creates an opportunity for Budgie to help users build consistent financial behavior through automated expense tracking, customizable daily budgets, and real-time purchase feedback.

About Budgie

A finance app that supports behavior.

Budgie is a smart budgeting app designed to help users track expenses, organize spending, and understand the impact of their purchases in real time.

The app reduces the friction of manual expense tracking by automatically recording and categorizing spending. It gives users clear insights into where their money goes, alerts them when they are close to budget limits, and helps them pause before making unnecessary purchases.

Core idea

A low-effort budgeting app that helps users stay consistent, understand their spending, and make smarter purchase decisions before they pay.

Value Proposition

Daily financial control, without the effort.

Budgie helps users stay in control of their daily spending by turning budgeting into an automated, real-time, and effortless experience.

Unlike traditional financial apps that only show users what they spent after the fact, Budgie helps users make smarter decisions before overspending happens.

Automated tracking Through iOS Shortcuts and SMS notifications.

Our Process

From behavior problem to product solution.

01

Understanding the Problem

We explored why budgeting is challenging for many. The main issues include lack of awareness, consistency, discipline, and supportive systems for better spending.

02

Research & Insights

Our research revealed that expense management is primarily a behavioral issue. Users need support before they spend, not just afterward.

03

Defining the Opportunity

Saudi Arabia’s growing fintech sector and rising digital payment habits created an opportunity for a localized budgeting app that fits daily financial behavior.

04

Designing the Solution

We designed Budgie to be visual, simple, and easy to use. The app shows income, spending, remaining balance, categories, and insights clearly.

05

Supporting Better Habits

Budgie keeps users informed through alerts, spending insights, and guidance on purchase impact without making users feel judged or overwhelmed.

Style Guides

Friendly, clear, and financially stable.

Visual Direction

The color system reflects freshness, movement, and quick action. The dark and neutral colors create contrast and keep the design modern and clear.

Typography

SF Pro Display is used for titles and important headings because it is clean, bold, and easy to scan.

SF Pro Rounded gives the app a softer and more approachable personality, making financial management feel less intimidating.

Primary Colors

Blue tones communicate stability, trust, clarity, and financial awareness.

Neutral Colors

Neutral shades support readability, contrast, and a clean app structure.

Margins, Sizes, and Corners

Spacing in Budgie follows multiples of 8 to keep the layout consistent and organized. Corner radius follows multiples of 2, creating a 1:4 relationship between corners and spacing.

Images

The visual elements are inspired by the budgie bird, which acts as the app’s mascot. The mascot gives the app a friendly and supportive personality.
