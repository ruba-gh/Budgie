Our story

Why we built Budgie around everyday habits

Managing money is not always difficult because people do not care. Tracking expenses takes effort, spending decisions happen quickly, and overspending can become clear only after the money is gone. Budgie was created for adults in Saudi Arabia who want greater consistency and awareness in their daily spending. Our early research with 114 participants helped shape a simple, low-effort budgeting experience.

Behind the app

From a spending challenge to an iPhone app

Team 16 brought research, design, and development together to build Budgie. The interface uses SwiftUI, with iOS Shortcuts supporting bank SMS transaction imports. We focused on clear categories, daily spending insights, and widgets that help make budgeting part of an everyday routine.

Inside Budgie

A clearer picture of where your money goes

Budgie combines customizable budgets, spending categories, automatic tracking through iOS Shortcuts, and daily, weekly, and monthly insights. Widgets offer quick budget visibility without opening the app. The goal is to help you understand your spending and approach your next purchase with greater awareness.
