01

How this website uses storage

The Budgie website’s own code does not set cookies or include advertising and analytics trackers. It uses one local-storage preference when you choose whether to reduce motion. Local storage stays in your browser; it is not automatically attached to web requests like a cookie.

02

What is saved

Setting Purpose Duration
budgie-reduce-motion Remembers your choice to reduce website animations. It contains only true or false. Until you reset it, clear site data, or your browser removes it.

The setting is only written after you use the motion control. Without a saved choice, the website follows your device’s reduced-motion preference.

03

Manage your preference

Use the control below to delete Budgie’s saved animation choice and return to your device’s setting. This does not change or delete any data in the Budgie iPhone app.

Animation preference

Your browser’s setting is used unless you have saved a choice.

04

Browser controls

Your browser settings can delete or block local storage and cookies for this site. Blocking storage does not prevent you from reading the website; the animation preference may simply stop persisting between visits.

05

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver the website. See our Privacy policy for details. If you follow an external link, that service may use its own cookies and storage according to its own policy.

06

Changes to this notice

This notice reflects the website’s current implementation. If new storage or tracking features are added, this page should describe them before they are enabled.
