01

Who and what this policy covers

This notice describes the Budgie informational website maintained by Team 16. It covers browsing this website and its local display preference. It does not describe the iPhone app’s handling of transactions, SMS imports, accounts, or other app data. Please review the privacy information provided with the app before using it.

02

Information on this website

The website does not ask you to create an account, enter bank information, upload files, or submit a contact form. Its own scripts do not read your SMS, access bank accounts, or collect budget transactions. The financial values visible in screenshots are example interface content.

03

Website delivery and hosting

When your browser loads this website, the hosting service receives the requests needed to deliver its pages and images. GitHub, which provides GitHub Pages hosting, describes technical and usage information such as IP addresses and request details in its privacy statement. Its retention and processing practices are governed by that statement.

04

Your display preference

If you use the “Reduce motion” control, the website saves your choice in your browser’s local storage. This preference is used only to choose how page animations behave. The website does not send this stored choice to a Budgie server. You can remove it from the Cookies page or through your browser settings.

05

Analytics and advertising

The website’s code does not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts, or embedded social feeds. Images, styles, and scripts are served with the site. External links only open when you choose them; the destination’s privacy practices then apply.

06

Your choices

You can browse without entering personal information, follow your device’s reduced-motion setting, and clear the saved display preference at any time. For information about data processed by the hosting provider and available requests, use the contact and rights information in its privacy statement.

07

Questions and updates

For questions about the Budgie website, contact the website maintainer. Do not include financial records or bank credentials in social messages. This notice will need to be updated if the website adds forms, analytics, or other data-processing features.
